Wall, roof and chimney pieces from Kenney's Fantasy Town Kit
(https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit), CC0 (see License.txt).
The pieces are unchanged; only those Railmaster uses are kept.

Railmaster assembles town buildings from them: tools/models/town_prefabs.py
writes ../prefabs.json. Textures/colormap-slate.png and
colormap-terracotta.png are period recolourings of the kit's palette
(teal roofs to slate or red-brown tile, lavender stone to grey), made by
tools/models/period_palettes.py.
