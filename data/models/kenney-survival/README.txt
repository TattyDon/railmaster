Logs, stumps, a tent, a sawbench, stone and ore, rocks, barrels and crates
from Kenney's Survival Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/survival-kit), CC0 (see
License.txt). The models are unchanged; only those Railmaster uses are kept.

Textures/colormap-weathered.png is a recolouring of the kit's palette (its
bright orange wood to weathered brown), made by
tools/models/period_palettes.py --survival. The prop clusters in
../prefabs.json (tools/models/town_prefabs.py) use it.
