Works, tanks, a cooling tower and a water tower from Kenney's City Kit
(Industrial) (https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial), CC0 (see
License.txt). The models are unchanged; only those Railmaster uses are kept.

Textures/colormap-brick.png is a period recolouring of the kit's palette
(pale walls to red brick, blue-grey to slate and iron, orange to rust), made
by tools/models/period_palettes.py --industrial; the prefabs in
../prefabs.json (tools/models/town_prefabs.py) use it.
